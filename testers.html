Information for testers

Please read this before agreeing to test. Last updated 21 November 2026.

Before you agree

  • It is a test version. It may have faults, including ones that stop it speaking. It is not a medical device and has not been reviewed by a speech-language pathologist. The starting words and pictures are a draft.
  • Keep your child's current way of communicating available at all times: device, book or signs. Don't let this app replace it during the test.
  • If your child works with a speech-language pathologist or therapist, tell them you are trying it.
  • Test versions expire 90 days after they are made. A newer one usually arrives before then, but that isn't guaranteed.
  • Privacy: the app keeps everything on the iPad and does not use the internet. Read the privacy policy.
  • What we ask: use it when it suits you, and report problems with "Report a problem". We don't watch or record your child, and we don't need your child's name.
  • You can stop at any time by deleting the app.

To take part, reply "I agree" to your invitation message.

Installing

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store on the iPad.
  2. Open the invitation email on that iPad and tap "Accept", then "Install".
  3. TestFlight doesn't work with a child's Apple account (one managed through Family Sharing for a child). Install on an iPad signed in with an adult's Apple account.
  4. On first launch, choose a board size. Buttons stay where they are once chosen.

To open the adult area, hold the lock button for 2 seconds, then type the four numbers shown as words.

Reporting a problem

In the app, open the adult area, then ⋯ → Report a problem. Write what happened and what you expected. The screen shows everything the report contains before you share it, and the report has no words, board names, photos or recordings. Tap Share and send it to [contact address — to be added], or tap Copy and paste it into a message.

Please don't send screenshots that show your child's photos, name or face.

The problems that matter most to us: the app didn't speak, said the wrong thing or was slow to speak; a change was lost; or a child got into the adult area.
