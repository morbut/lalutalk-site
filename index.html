LaluTalk

A free picture-communication (AAC) app for young children, for iPad. In testing.

Children tap pictures and the iPad speaks. An adult sets it up and changes it behind a lock. It works without the internet and keeps everything on the iPad.

This is a test version. It is not a medical device and has not been reviewed by a speech-language pathologist. Keep your child's current way of communicating available.
